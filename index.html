Profile

Controls and mechanical design, taken all the way to hardware.

I'm Owen Tharp, a junior at Webb School of Knoxville, and I spend most of my time on the part of engineering where the model meets the metal. I am Controls/Systems Director for FRC Team 1466, where I lead the autonomous stack; over summer 2026 I was a CAD engineering intern at Formall Inc., taking an automated plug-assist platform from an assigned end goal to a released 70-sheet drawing package.

My favorite projects are the ones that force the whole loop closed. The Furuta pendulum above is the clearest example: I derived the dynamic model from CAD mass properties and test logs, tuned the LQR gains against hardware data, wrote the 200 Hz embedded controller, and then spent the real time debugging it on the bench until it actually stood up.

I also like teaching it. I run a five-student programming group on 1466 with eight technical decks I wrote myself, mentored a 15-student cohort at Cisco Hack Camp, and have taught roughly forty lower-school students at Camp Webb.

Teensy 4.1 · ODrive S1 · magnetic encoders · aluminum extrusion
